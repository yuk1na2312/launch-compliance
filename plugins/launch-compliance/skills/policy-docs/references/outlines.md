# Document outlines

Use these as section checklists, not boilerplate. Every section must be filled from the facts sheet or the user's answers; drop sections that don't apply and say so in your hand-over message.

## Privacy policy

1. Who we are - operator name, country, contact email (and DPO/representative if the user has one)
2. What we collect - by category, matching the facts sheet; include data from third parties (e.g. OAuth profile)
3. How we use it - purposes; for EU/UK users, the legal basis per purpose (contract, legitimate interests, consent, legal obligation)
4. Who we share it with - every vendor by name and role; say you don't sell personal data only if true (ad pixels can count as "sharing" under CPRA)
5. AI processing - provider, what is sent, retention/training terms the user confirmed
6. International transfers - where vendors process data; transfer safeguards
7. Cookies and similar technologies - summary plus link to the cookie policy
8. How long we keep it - per category
9. Your rights and how to use them - access, correction, deletion, export, objection, withdraw consent, complaint to authority; the real mechanism and response time
10. Children - minimum age; what happens if a child's data is found
11. Security - only measures that are true
12. Changes to this policy - how users are notified
13. Region-specific sections - EU/UK, California, Vietnam (as applicable)
14. Contact
15. Last updated

## Terms of service

1. Agreement and eligibility - acceptance, minimum age
2. Accounts - responsibility for credentials, accurate information
3. The service - what it is; changes and availability; beta features
4. Payments and subscriptions - prices, billing cycle, renewal, trials, taxes, cancellation (link refund policy) - only if payments exist
5. Acceptable use - prohibited conduct relevant to the product
6. User content - ownership, license granted to operate the service, moderation, takedown - only if UGC exists
7. AI-generated output - may be inaccurate; user responsibility; ownership of outputs as far as the provider's terms allow - only if AI features exist
8. Intellectual property - the operator's rights; open-source and third-party components
9. Third-party services - links and integrations
10. Termination - by user (and how), by operator
11. Disclaimers and limitation of liability - with the note that consumer-protection laws may limit these
12. Governing law and disputes - from user
13. Changes to the terms
14. Contact

## Refund policy

1. Scope - which products/plans
2. Subscriptions - cancellation effect (end of period vs immediate), refunds for partial periods, renewals
3. One-time purchases - window and conditions
4. Free trials - when billing starts, reminder emails if any
5. EU/UK right of withdrawal - 14 days; digital content exception only with express consent and acknowledgment at checkout
6. How to request - email or self-serve, information needed, processing time
7. Chargebacks - ask users to contact you first (neutral tone, no threats)
8. Contact

## Cookie policy

1. What cookies and similar technologies are
2. Categories used - strictly necessary, preferences, analytics, marketing (only those present)
3. Table - name or provider, category, purpose, duration, first/third party
4. How to manage choices - link/button to reopen the consent banner; browser settings
5. Changes and contact
